# Rome in 5 days

Five days in **Rome**, staying central and walking most of it. One big sight a day at most — the city is better when you leave room for the streets between the sights.

**Dates:** 12–16 May · **Base:** Monti · **Getting around:** on foot, metro when tired

## Logistics

### Flights

| Date | Route | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 12 May | Milan Linate (LIN) → Rome Fiumicino (FCO) | 09:15 → 10:25 |
| 16 May | Rome Fiumicino (FCO) → Milan Linate (LIN) | 19:30 → 20:40 |

### Getting in from the airport

The Leonardo Express runs Fiumicino → Termini in about 30 minutes, twice an hour. From Termini the flat is a fifteen-minute walk.

Land 10:25, train to Termini, drop the bags. No monuments today — walk Monti, find a coffee, adjust.

## Notes for next time

- Five days covers the centre comfortably; Ostia and the Appia need their own day and didn't fit.
- May was the right call — warm, long evenings, and the queues still manageable.
