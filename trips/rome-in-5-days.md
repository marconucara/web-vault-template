# Rome in 5 days

Five days in **Rome**, staying central and walking most of it. One big sight a day at most — the city is better when you leave room for the streets between the sights.

**Dates:** 12–16 May · **Base:** Monti · **Getting around:** on foot, metro when tired

## Logistics

### Flights

| Date | Route | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 12 May | Milan Linate (LIN) → Rome Fiumicino (FCO) | 09:15 → 10:25 |
| 16 May | Rome Fiumicino (FCO) → Milan Linate (LIN) | 19:30 → 20:40 |

### Getting in from the airport

The Leonardo Express runs Fiumicino → Termini in about 30 minutes, twice an hour. From Termini the flat is a fifteen-minute walk.

### Where we sleep

- [The flat](https://maps.app.goo.gl/PKUQKKWjZjHBZjSVA) top floor on Via dei Serpenti, keys from the caffè downstairs

### Restaurants

- [La Carbonara](https://maps.app.goo.gl/kpGGKXWCVzvkrnUt9) two streets from the flat, no bookings, go early
- [Roscioli](https://maps.app.goo.gl/sdFGzACp9YoxfphQ9) deli at the front, dining room behind — worth the fuss, **book ahead**
- [Da Enzo](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Ni7RhMzL8VeLEAbH9) tiny, in Trastevere, expect to queue
- [Pizzarium](https://maps.app.goo.gl/kdMi6MeGrjzrvEnh6) pizza al taglio by weight, standing only — near the Vatican

### Museums and sights

- [Colosseum](https://maps.app.goo.gl/LyQhJpcdTUQmnLjs6) the one everyone comes for — timed entry only
- [Roman Forum](https://maps.app.goo.gl/hg2A2YN3Fjx6T1tq6) same ticket as the Colosseum, little shade
- [Vatican Museums](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Ybr5w1B88HsA361S7) book a slot, and go for the *first* one of the day
- [St. Peter's](https://maps.app.goo.gl/GD1dbjrrVMPBab75A) free to enter — the dome climb is a separate ticket
- [Pantheon](https://maps.app.goo.gl/KTkQqFfAjDBM7ogZA) five minutes inside is enough, but go when it rains
- [Piazza Navona](https://maps.app.goo.gl/BJSbg6ZtHNmD37bL6)
- [Trevi Fountain](https://maps.app.goo.gl/JJQ8yHqKzYhfZRrd7) packed at every hour — early morning is the only quiet one
- [Galleria Borghese](https://maps.app.goo.gl/RuadYDzq84vC51Dx8) two-hour slots, sells out weeks ahead
- [Trastevere](https://maps.app.goo.gl/zEKeWRvezhKtNPac9) not a sight, just the best evening walk

## Day by day

### Day 1 — Tue 12 May · Arrival and the neighbourhood

Land 10:25, train to Termini, drop the bags. No monuments today — walk Monti, find a coffee, adjust.

1. [The flat](https://maps.app.goo.gl/PKUQKKWjZjHBZjSVA) drop the bags, then straight back out
2. [La Carbonara](https://maps.app.goo.gl/kpGGKXWCVzvkrnUt9) dinner, no booking — 19:30 or queue

### Day 2 — Wed 13 May · Ancient Rome

The Colosseum and the Forum in one morning, early, before the queues and the heat. The afternoon is deliberately empty: that is enough walking on stone.

1. [Colosseum](https://maps.app.goo.gl/LyQhJpcdTUQmnLjs6) 08:30 slot, tickets already bought — €18 each, Forum included
2. [Roman Forum](https://maps.app.goo.gl/MVacVnRCsWjHoNDx9) straight on from the Colosseum, same ticket
3. [Roscioli](https://maps.app.goo.gl/sdFGzACp9YoxfphQ9) 13:30, booked — the deli counter does takeaway if we run late

### Day 3 — Thu 14 May · The Vatican

1. [Vatican Museums](https://maps.app.goo.gl/Ybr5w1B88HsA361S7) 09:00 entry, Sistine Chapel at the end of the route
2. [St. Peter's](https://maps.app.goo.gl/GD1dbjrrVMPBab75A) straight through from the Sistine Chapel exit — *shoulders covered*
3. [Pizzarium](https://maps.app.goo.gl/kdMi6MeGrjzrvEnh6) late lunch standing up, ten minutes north
4. [Trastevere](https://maps.app.goo.gl/EDnKLJstMGVrzTmG8) cross the river for the evening
5. [Da Enzo](https://maps.app.goo.gl/33fTYShpfPdoCKe16) dinner if the queue is under half an hour

### Day 4 — Fri 15 May · Centro storico

All within twenty minutes of each other and best without a plan. Wander, stop when something looks good.

1. [Trevi Fountain](https://maps.app.goo.gl/9GDGziBdY7r6qPrp6) first thing, before it fills up
2. [Pantheon](https://maps.app.goo.gl/v9Ww1dyS5rX1CBuc7)
3. [Piazza Navona](https://maps.app.goo.gl/7GDGCDY9dk8G6ctF6) coffee at a table, watch the square
4. [Galleria Borghese](https://maps.app.goo.gl/jyjywwx49bxbmCg38) 17:00 slot — the last one, and the quietest

### Day 5 — Sat 16 May · Home

- Morning free: one last coffee, whatever we missed.
- Train back to Fiumicino by 17:00 for the 19:30 flight.

## Notes for next time

- Five days covers the centre comfortably; Ostia and the Appia need their own day and didn't fit.
- May was the right call — warm, long evenings, and the queues still manageable.
