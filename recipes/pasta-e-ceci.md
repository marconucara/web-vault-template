# Pasta e ceci

Store-cupboard dinner, half an hour, better the second day. This is the version
that works — ignore the ones that skip the rosemary.

## Ingredients

- 200 g chickpeas, cooked, with a ladle of their liquid
- 150 g short pasta, mixed shapes are traditional
- 1 sprig rosemary
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- Olive oil, salt, black pepper

## Method

1. Warm the oil with the garlic and rosemary until fragrant; don't colour the
   garlic. Fish both out.
2. Stir in the tomato paste, then the chickpeas and their liquid. Simmer ten
   minutes and crush about a third of them against the pan.
3. Add the pasta and enough water to cover. Cook it in there, stirring — it
   should end up loose, not soupy and not dry.
4. Off the heat, plenty of pepper and a hard pour of raw oil.

## Notes

Tested twice. Holds overnight but thickens a lot — loosen with hot water, not
more oil.
