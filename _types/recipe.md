# Recipe

One recipe from your own cookbook. The point of keeping them here is that they
win: when a recipe in this vault disagrees with one you found online, this is
the version you actually cook.

- **category** — how you group them (pasta, bread, dessert…).
- **status** — tested and trusted, or still to confirm.
- **yield** — servings or quantity.
