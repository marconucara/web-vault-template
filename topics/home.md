# Home

Everything to do with the flat, indefinitely. Not a project — there is no
version of this that gets finished and archived. Things start here, sometimes
grow into a project of their own, and come back here when they're done.

## What lives under this

- [[kitchen-renovation]] — the one active project right now.
- Property tax, due at the end of the year. Same deadline, same paperwork, every
  year; the note is worth keeping between rounds.
- Boiler service, its date and who to call.
- Anything else the flat asks for: a leak, a quote, the neighbour's building
  work.

## Why it's a Topic and not a Project

A project has a goal and an end — the kitchen is done when the kitchen is done.
Home has neither. It's the thread that connects the kitchen to next year's tax
bill to whatever breaks in the spring, none of which belong to each other except
that they all happen here.

That's what the **Topic** type is for: a subject that outlives the projects
inside it, and a place for the things too small to be projects at all.
